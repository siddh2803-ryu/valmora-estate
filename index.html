Architecture & Craft

How we build

Architecture

Every project here starts with the land itself. Of the twenty-nine farmhouses standing when the work began, most were little more than walls and sky. Twenty-two have now been brought back

For three decades the estate studio has held to the same habits: reclaimed materials where they can be found, the old techniques where they still serve, and the quiet machinery of modern comfort hidden well out of sight

A house returned to its hill

Materials

Working with what the land gives

Stone from the old terraces, chestnut from the wood, lime washed by hand — new work is folded into old until the seam disappears

See the houses
Drag

Inside

Interior craft

The buildings and their rooms are drawn by the same hand, so every table, latch and lamp knows the wall it was made for

Most of what fills the houses is knowledge within a day's drive — by the weavers, smiths and potters the studio has worked beside for a generation

the long view

The team that draws the houses also keeps them, year after year — and that is where the knowledge lives: in what lasts, what mellows and what was worth doing slowly

Private houses across the estate

Villas to Rent

Four houses, each with its own view

Each farmhouse has been returned to life over years of careful work. They share the same quiet standards — stone, timber, linen, and the long light of the valley — but each has its own character, its own corner of the estate

Highest of the four

Miradouro

Three bedrooms, a stone terrace, and a view that runs the whole length of the valley to the river bend below

Discover more

Among the old olives

Oliveira

Two bedrooms set within a grove of trees that were old when the house was first built. A shaded courtyard for the hottest afternoons

Discover more

At the vineyard's edge

Vinha

Four bedrooms in the heart of the working vineyard. Wake to the sound of harvest and the smell of crushed grape on the wind

Discover more

Closest to the water

Ribeira

Two bedrooms and a private path to the river. A small stone beach and the sound of water moving over rock
